## High

### R-1 [Security] — A sales rep can take over a customer's account by changing its email
- What a customer/staff member/the owner experiences, or what it risks or costs: A rep can edit the email of any customer on their list to an address the rep controls, then use "email me a code" to sign in as that customer and replace their password. The site already forbids reps from resetting such customers' passwords; this is a way around that rule.
- Severity: High   · Size: S   · Touches DB schema, money, checkout or sign-in: yes
- Where: `src/db/customerQueries.ts:268`
- Fix: A rep may change the email only of an account they created that has not yet verified an address or chosen its own password; every other email change is the admin's.
- Status: fixed (eefa517)

### R-2 [Security] — Anyone can freeze stock for free with a fake receipt
- What a customer/staff member/the owner experiences, or what it risks or costs: A visitor checks out as a guest, uploads any tiny image as a "receipt", and the order then holds its stock with no deadline and cannot be cancelled by the customer; only staff can release it. Repeated, it empties the shelf for real buyers.
- Severity: High   · Size: M   · Touches DB schema, money, checkout or sign-in: yes
- Where: `src/db/paymentProofQueries.ts:57`
- Fix: Give "confirming payment" its own deadline after which stock is released (or the order cancelled) if staff have not confirmed.
- Status: needs owner decision — when a receipt is uploaded but staff have not confirmed it, after how many days (if ever) should the site release the held stock on its own?

### R-3 [Cost] — Category "list view" pages are open to crawlers and each one is a database read
- What a customer/staff member/the owner experiences, or what it risks or costs: The same pattern that pushed the database over its monthly quota in October: about 340 list pages per language, none cached, each linked from a category page, and the deepest ones sort 12,900 rows per visit. One crawler wave can repeat the overage.
- Severity: High   · Size: S   · Touches DB schema, money, checkout or sign-in: no
- Where: `public/robots.txt:38`
- Fix: Disallow `/en/l/*?` and `/fa/l/*?` in robots.txt, as is done for filtered family pages.
- Status: fixed (b254a2c)
